THE LETTERS

A familiar family,
a different finish.

Miracle Grove adapts Fraunces by Undercase Type, designed by Phaedra Charles and Flavia Zimbardi. Most of the underlying letterforms remain theirs.

This edition adds a returning foot to the upright lowercase l and related characters. Fixed optical settings, tabular figures, and real italics make up the eight-style family.

A few things to know.

This is primarily a display family. Test small text at its actual size. The fonts cover Latin characters, with English and Spanish samples tested; they do not cover every writing system.
